# Problem and segments

All content below is hypothesis unless labelled verified. No source outside the repository was used. Register IDs refer to `assumptions-register.md`.

## Problem statement

Verified (`CLAUDE.md` §2, 2026-11-06): the product premise is that sewing enterprises in Kyrgyzstan have textile leftovers and ARTA helps them get economic value from them.

Everything else is a hypothesis:

| # | Hypothesis | Label | ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Sewing enterprises produce textile offcuts and leftover rolls regularly | assumption | A-01 |
| P2 | Most of it is discarded, stored or sold informally, not systematically sold | to-validate | A-02 |
| P3 | Enterprises do not know who could use their offcuts, or at what price | assumption | A-03 |
| P4 | Disposal or storage costs the enterprise money or space | to-validate | A-04 |
| P5 | Volume per enterprise (kg per month) | to-validate: `___` | A-05 |
| P6 | Typical material mix (fabric type, size of pieces, mixed colours) | to-validate | A-06 |

## Segments

For each segment: pain, willingness to pay, reachability and value capture are all hypotheses.

### 1. Sewing enterprises (seller; role `enterprise`)

| Item | Hypothesis | Label / ID |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Unused offcuts take space, have no known buyer, bring no income | assumption A-03 |
| Willingness to pay | Would pay a share only if a real sale happens | assumption A-07 |
| Reachability | Unknown; no contact list exists | to-validate A-17 |
| Value capture | Commission on completed sales (5% hypothesis); possible internal-use savings shown as forecast | assumption A-08 |
| Interview questions | What is left over per month, how stored, who takes it, at what price, what stops you selling it | to-validate |

### 2. Workshops and small makers (buyer; role `buyer`)

| Item | Hypothesis | Label / ID |
|---|---|---|

## Delivery

Delivery cost and who arranges it are unknown. The MVP has no delivery routing (`CLAUDE.md` §2); cost per kg is an explicit input or a marked assumption (A-14).

## Minimum validation before judging (fits the 48 hour window)

- 5 to 10 short interviews: at least 3 sewing enterprises, 3 workshops, 1 recycler.
- Record: volume per month, current destination, price per kg, delivery arrangement, willingness to pay commission, who decides.
- Until done, the pitch must describe these points as hypotheses and must not say "we interviewed".
